Unconventional Reflections

Sat 10 November 2018

Filed under misc

Tags Poems

I find myself most alluring in the rear-view mirror Just one eye, very blue, sliced neatly out of my face. I’m always charmed at the sight, Myself observing myself observing myself.

Or in a bus window, at night When I can see myself reflected all wobbly in the plastic, Disappearing as we drive under streetlights. I can pretend I’m a stranger, Alluring in the way only strangers on the bus can be Where I can see invisible futures together stretching out ahead of us Intangible, impossible, incalculable.

I don’t like feeling seen. I don’t like when the strangers on the bus look at me, how they might, as I do, think about the strange complexities of life And contemplate the infinite paths that might take the two of us together Or thinking about who I am, what I’m like, how I spend my time, As I do, when looking at other strangers who silently share my bus ride.

I don’t like the slide of men’s eyes down my body When I walk down the street I think about plucking their eyes from their skull, Stealing a piece of them, just as they have stolen some piece of me.

All they have ever done is take, and take, and take.

If I could snap my fingers and make every man Who ever called out on a street corner Or whistled in the middle of the night Or touched a woman who didn’t ask for it Or followed a woman who didn’t want it If I could take every man who has ever raked his vile eyes up and down my body And make them all vanish, in an instant I would.
