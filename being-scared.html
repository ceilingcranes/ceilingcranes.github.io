Being Scared

Sun 16 December 2018

Filed under Poems

The anxiety rakes its claws down the inside of my chest.
It feels like being flayed
Like a fillet (filet?)
My heart squeeze squeeze squeezing away
Or like a shudder
A stumble in my words or
A stutter in my step
A missed stair scooping breath out of me

It’s like a little whisper saying
It’s like a voice muttering in my
It’s an undercurrent, to my every thought, under every smile and biting laugh
Every math equation, every paragraph
Circling like sharks, or like buzzards,
Or like a metaphor that’s never quite there,
Whispering
you aren’t good enough
And worse, so much worse
you never will be

I joke about it, right, cracking jokes like when I get myself lost in a building I know well, or when I can’t remember a friend’s birthday, or when I’m wrong about something goddammit i thought i could do this one thing-
So when people say the other thing (you’re smart) I hear it as a joke, which I recognize doesn’t make that much sense, because when I say it (i’m a moron) I’m not really joking, right? But if they’re not joking, then they might actually think it, and man, wouldn’t that be worse? That would be worse for me, I think, because shit, what am I supposed to do then?

I try to attach words to it
oh what like youre some kind of writer or something how cute
Which I guess kinda maybe sorta makes it less
I always hate it when people attach words to me
So maybe it’s my little way of fighting back
Sweeping the flashlight beam around the room and saying
“Oh, silly me, it was just an oddly placed coat
Or a chair
Or a mirror
I scared myself for nothing!”
